# Wizard Bet — 100 Playable Demo Games

A static, mobile-first demo with 100 original game entries and local virtual-credit gameplay.

Game mechanics:
- 10 Slots
- 10 Crash
- 10 Wheel
- 10 Dice
- 10 Roulette
- 10 Mines
- 10 Blackjack
- 10 Plinko
- 10 Keno
- 10 Penalty

No real-money deposits, withdrawals, payment processing, or gambling transactions are included.
